# Business Problem

## Background

The company operates across sales, manufacturing, and import-export activities. It manages customer orders across domestic and international markets, with multiple products, shipping methods, payment conditions, and order statuses.

The business has operational sales data but needs a structured way to understand sales performance, profitability, customer contribution, geographic performance, and order delivery efficiency.

## Problem Statement

Management needs better visibility into sales and operational performance to identify profitable products and customers, understand regional performance, monitor order and delivery issues, and identify areas requiring management attention.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales and operational order data and develop a data-driven view of business performance.

The analysis will focus on:

- Sales and revenue performance
- Profitability and profit margins
- Customer contribution
- Product performance
- Regional and country performance
- Order status and cancellations
- Delivery and shipping performance
- Payment status

## Key Business Questions

### Sales Performance

1. What is the total revenue generated?
2. What is the total profit generated?
3. What are the monthly sales trends?
4. Which products generate the highest revenue?
5. Which customers contribute the most revenue?

### Profitability

6. Which products generate the highest profit?
7. Which products have high revenue but relatively low profit margins?
8. Which product categories are most profitable?

### Geographic Performance

9. Which countries generate the most revenue?
10. Which regions have the strongest profitability?

### Operational Performance

11. How many orders are completed, pending, cancelled, or returned?
12. How many orders are delivered late?
13. Which shipping methods experience more delays?
14. Which regions have weaker delivery performance?

### Payment Performance

15. How many orders have pending or overdue payments?
16. Which customers contribute the largest amount of pending or overdue business?

## Expected Outcome

The project will produce an analytical dashboard and supporting analysis that allow management to monitor key performance indicators, identify business trends, and focus attention on areas requiring further investigation.

The final analysis will translate the findings into practical business recommendations.

## Dataset

The dataset used in this project is a realistic synthetic dataset created for portfolio and learning purposes. It represents sales and operational transactions for a fictional manufacturing and import-export business.

The dataset should not be interpreted as actual company data.
