# Research Objectives

## Working Research Title

An Empirical Analysis of Sales and Operational Factors Associated with Customer Satisfaction in Brazilian E-Commerce

## Research Question

How are delivery performance, freight cost, order characteristics, and product categories associated with customer satisfaction in Brazilian e-commerce?

## Objectives

1. To analyze sales and order patterns across the available observation period.

2. To examine product and product-category performance using order-item price and freight information.

3. To evaluate order fulfillment and delivery performance using order status and delivery timestamps.

4. To examine the association between delivery performance and customer review scores.

5. To analyze payment methods, payment installments, and order payment values.

6. To investigate customer purchasing behavior using unique customer identifiers and order frequency.

7. To examine geographic patterns in customer and seller activity.

8. To identify operational and sales patterns that may provide useful insights for e-commerce decision-making.

## Scope

The analysis focuses on the historical Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset covering orders placed between 2016 and 2018.

The study is observational and analytical. Findings will describe patterns and associations within the dataset and will not be interpreted as causal relationships unless supported by an appropriate analytical design.

## Key Analytical Dimensions

- Sales and order volume
- Product categories
- Customer behavior
- Payment methods
- Freight value
- Delivery performance
- Delivery delays
- Customer review scores
- Geography
